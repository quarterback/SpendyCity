# PDX Spend — Voice Guide

## The rules

**Say it once.** The thesis lives in the hero. No section re-explains it. If the visual answers it, the prose doesn't.

**Lead with specifics.** Open every page with a fund name, a dollar amount, a year, a person, a neighborhood. Not "public funding measures" or "an editorial accounting."

**No announced absences.** Never tell the reader what the site won't do, isn't, or doesn't include. Cut "there are no calls to action," "this is not a live ledger," "should not be cited as such."

**No contrastive scaffolding.** The pattern "X is not Y" disappears from body copy. If a definition is needed, state it positively. "Sold as X but now Y" → cut.

**Sentences ≤ 25 words.** No paragraph stacks more than three clauses joined by semicolons or dashes.

**Headings ≤ 6 words, assertive.** A heading should assert something, not label a region. "What you are looking at" → cut. "Promise versus delivery" → keep (it asserts).

**"Modeled" appears sparingly.** At most twice on the landing page, at most once per fund page in body prose. Charts carry a small `MODELED` badge — that's the disclosure mechanism. The methodology page defines the term once.

**Margin notes earn their space.** Each note either (a) cites a source, (b) defines a term the body doesn't explain, or (c) adds a number or fact not in the adjacent body. Notes that paraphrase the body are deleted.

**Chart subtitles: one sentence.** Plain words. No re-explaining colors the legend already labels. No naming the modeling. Example shape: "Year-end balance, 2014–2024."

**Share text matches body voice.** No "an editorial accounting of…" or "a structural pattern."
